What we do

Services

PCB & Hardware Design

Custom printed circuit board layout and schematic capture. From simple breakouts to complex multi-layer boards with tight impedance requirements.

Embedded Systems Design

Microcontroller selection, peripheral integration, RTOS configuration, and BSP development. Architected for reliability and maintainability.

Firmware Development

Bare-metal and RTOS-based firmware in C/C++. Driver development, communication stacks (I2C, SPI, UART, CAN, USB), and OTA update systems.

CAD — Enclosures & Sheet Metal

3D modelling of custom enclosures, brackets, and sheet metal parts. DFM-ready designs for injection moulding, CNC, and off-the-shelf fabrication.
